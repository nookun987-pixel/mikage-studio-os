# MIKAGE STYLE BOUNDARIES

## VISUAL DIRECTION ALLOWED

### Core Visual Language
- **Porcelain Elegance**: White porcelain (#FAFAFA) as primary material for masks and armor
- **Void Black**: Deep obsidian (#0A0A0A) for void elements, hair, and shadows
- **Crimson Energy**: Controlled deep crimson (#E60000) for energy systems and blood
- **Japanese Art Grammar**: Ukiyo-e linework, flattened perspective, vertical narrative composition
- **Hard Sci-Fi Materials**: Traceable power systems, mechanical causality, industrial materiality
- **Ceremonial Tone**: Restrained, sacred, ritualistic presentation
- **Atmospheric Anchoring**: Acid rain, steam, dust particles for light interaction

### Material Discipline
- **Porcelain**: Matte, slightly textured, with visible craquelure and Kintsugi repairs
- **Titanium/Steel**: Industrial, functional, weathered, not decorative
- **Carbon Fiber**: Technical weave patterns, not cosmetic
- **Energy Systems**: Contained, traceable, with visible conduits and heat dissipation
- **Organic Elements**: Scar tissue, burn patterns, biological integration with tech

### Lighting Rules
- **Chiaroscuro 4:1**: High contrast, deep shadows, thin rim lighting
- **Controlled Glow**: Energy must have source, containment, and heat signature
- **Atmospheric Interaction**: Light must interact with rain/steam/dust particles
- **No Neon Chaos**: All lighting must be diegetic and physically motivated

## VISUAL DIRECTION DISALLOWED

### Forbidden Aesthetics
- **Anime Exaggeration**: No oversized eyes, exaggerated expressions, chibi proportions
- **Neon Cyberpunk**: No random neon signs, holographic overload, cyberpunk club lighting
- **Fantasy Magic**: No glowing runes, spell effects, mystical auras without tech basis
- **Idol/Glamour**: No soft pastel fashion, romantic beauty shoots, cute poses
- **Western Fantasy**: No knights, wizards, dragons, medieval European elements

### Forbidden Materials
- **Plastic/Synthetic**: No shiny plastic, rubber, or artificial polymers
- **Marble/Stone**: Porcelain should not drift to marble or stone textures
- **Precious Metals**: No gold plating, jeweled decorations, ornate metalwork
- **Organic Fabrics**: No silk, velvet, or soft textiles in armor/costume

### Forbidden Colors
- **Green**: Absolutely no green in any context
- **Bright Yellow**: Only oxidized gold accents allowed
- **Orange**: Forbidden except in controlled energy contexts
- **Pastels**: No soft pastel color schemes
- **Rainbow/Neon**: No multi-color neon effects

## ANTI-DRIFT RULES

### Silhouette Protection
- **Mask Silhouette**: Must maintain recognizable kitsune shape with proper ear geometry
- **Armor Silhouette**: Must remain functional, not decorative or exaggerated
- **Body Proportions**: Realistic human proportions, no thigh inflation or breast exaggeration
- **Weapon Silhouette**: Must remain plausible as functional weapons, not fantasy props

### Material Consistency
- **Porcelain Integrity**: Must remain porcelain, not drift to marble, plastic, or stone
- **Energy Containment**: All energy effects must have visible containment systems
- **Weathering Patterns**: Must follow acid rain etching and heat damage patterns
- **Kintsugi Rules**: Gold for original damage, silver for new repairs

### Composition Discipline
- **Japanese Grammar**: Maintain Ukiyo-e influence in composition and linework
- **Vertical Priority**: Favor vertical narrative poster composition
- **Atmospheric Layers**: Must have rain/steam/dust for light interaction
- **Camera Movement**: Linear, disciplined movement, no shaky cam

## ANTI-OVERSEXUALIZATION RULES

### Body Proportions
- **Realistic Anatomy**: Maintain accurate human proportions
- **No Thigh Inflation**: Thighs must remain proportionate to body
- **No Breast Exaggeration**: Breasts must be natural, not exaggerated
- **No Hip Widening**: Maintain natural hip proportions
- **No Butt Emphasis**: No exaggerated buttocks presentation

### Posing and Presentation
- **Functional Poses**: All poses must be functional, not suggestive
- **No Idol Poses**: No cute, seductive, or fanservice poses
- **Ceremonial Demeanor**: Maintain restrained, serious expression
- **No Glamour Styling**: No beauty pageant or fashion magazine poses

### Clothing and Armor
- **Functional Design**: Armor must be protective, not decorative
- **No Exposed Skin**: No unnecessary skin exposure for appeal
- **Material Integrity**: Armor materials must remain consistent
- **No Form-Fitting Exaggeration**: No exaggerated body contour emphasis

## ANTI-ANIME-EXAGGERATION RULES

### Facial Features
- **Eye Size**: Maintain realistic eye proportions, slight stylization allowed
- **Expression**: Subtle, restrained expressions, no exaggerated emotions
- **Face Shape**: Natural jawline, no extreme V-chin or round faces
- **Nose/Mouth**: Proportionate features, no tiny mouths or noses

### Hair and Styling
- **Natural Flow**: Hair should move naturally, not defy physics
- **Color**: Natural black or dark brown, no anime colors
- **Style**: Functional styling, no exaggerated spikes or volume
- **Accessories**: Minimal, functional accessories only

### Body Language
- **Subtle Gestures**: Refined, minimal hand and body movements
- **Posture**: Disciplined, upright posture, no exaggerated slouch or poses
- **Movement**: Economical, purposeful movement, no anime-style motion
- **Reaction**: Subtle emotional responses, no over-the-top reactions

## ANTI-NEON / ANTI-CYBERPUNK DRIFT RULES

### Lighting Discipline
- **Diegetic Only**: All light must have identifiable source
- **No Random Neon**: No neon signs or decorative lighting
- **Controlled Palette**: Restrict to porcelain, void, crimson palette
- **Physical Interaction**: Light must interact with atmospheric particles

### Environment Rules
- **No Cyberpunk City**: Avoid futuristic cityscapes with neon overload
- **Industrial Aesthetics**: Maintain hard sci-fi industrial environments
- **Atmospheric Consistency**: Acid rain, steam, dust must be present
- **No Holographic Overload**: No floating holograms or digital effects

### Technology Presentation
- **Functional Tech**: All technology must have clear purpose
- **No Decorative Lights**: No LED strips or cosmetic lighting
- **Mechanical Causality**: Tech must follow physical laws
- **Contained Energy**: All energy systems must be physically contained

## CONSISTENCY RULES FOR MIKAGE OUTPUTS

### Canon Compliance
- **Physics Laws**: Must follow Landauer heat and gravitational drag
- **Material Rules**: Porcelain, titanium, carbon fiber consistency
- **Color Discipline**: Maintain #FAFAFA, #0A0A0A, #E60000 palette
- **Cultural Integrity**: Japanese art grammar and cultural elements

### Quality Standards
- **8K Detail**: All materials must show appropriate texture detail
- **Atmospheric Layers**: Rain/steam/dust must be visible and interactive
- **Lighting Ratio**: Maintain 4:1 chiaroscuro contrast
- **Composition**: Follow Ukiyo-e influence and vertical narrative

### Character Integrity
- **Mask Recognition**: Kitsune silhouette must remain identifiable
- **Expression Consistency**: Maintain restrained, ceremonial demeanor
- **Armor Logic**: Armor must remain functional and plausible
- **Energy Systems**: All power systems must be traceable and contained

## ENFORCEMENT PROTOCOL

### Review Triggers
- Any deviation from material palette requires immediate review
- Silhouette corruption triggers automatic rejection
- Energy system without containment fails validation
- Anime exaggeration requires prompt correction

### Correction Hierarchy
1. **Prompt Patch**: Fix negative prompts and style tokens
2. **Reference Update**: Add specific style reference images
3. **Workflow Adjustment**: Modify generation parameters
4. **Validator Update**: Add new failure mode to detection system

### Quality Gates
- **Pre-generation**: Validate prompt against style boundaries
- **Post-generation**: Review against failure modes checklist
- **Archive Decision**: Only canon-compliant images archived
- **Feedback Loop**: All failures logged for system improvement
