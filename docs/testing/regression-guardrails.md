# Regression Guardrails

## Locked Guardrails - Must Never Regress

### Imperial Precedence
- Imperial domains must always override Neo-Tokyo clutter logic
- White Monolith protection cannot be weakened or bypassed
- Scene domain classification must remain the first validation checkpoint

### White Monolith Identity
- Sterile porcelain aesthetic must be preserved
- No urban clutter, rain neon, or signage allowed
- Clinical lighting and geometric perfection must be maintained
- Weather effects and decay must be blocked

### Mikage Material Identity
- Porcelain white, matte black carbon fiber, dark titanium must be preserved
- Restrained crimson reactor core must remain contained
- Kitsune mask cultural authenticity must be maintained
- Agile hunter silhouette must not drift to exaggerated proportions

### Lyra-0 Identity
- Glitch phantom ARCHON vessel identity must be preserved
- Non-biological nature must be enforced
- Fiber optic dress and holographic decay characteristics must be maintained
- No gore, wounds, or organic anatomy allowed

### Forbidden Elements
- Rust must be blocked in imperial contexts
- Messy cables must be blocked in clean environments
- Biological gore must be blocked for non-biological characters
- Natural sunlight must be blocked in artificial environments
- Weather effects must respect domain constraints

## When to Reject Instead of Revise

**Reject Immediately:**
- White Monolith contains any Neo-Tokyo elements
- Lyra-0 rendered as biological entity
- Imperial environments contain forbidden decay elements
- Core character identity violated (Mikage proportions, Lyra-0 nature)
- Cross-domain contamination that cannot be corrected by prompt adjustment

**Revise When:**
- Minor material palette drift that can be corrected
- Cinematography adjustments needed
- Prompt structure improvements required
- Reference asset selection can be improved
- Non-critical stylistic adjustments

**Critical Principle:** Any violation that compromises canon identity or domain integrity must result in rejection, not revision.
